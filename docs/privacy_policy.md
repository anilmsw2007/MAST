MAST Life OS ("we", "our", or "the App") is designed with a strict **Privacy-First, Offline-First** philosophy for Indian families. Your trust and privacy are our highest priorities.

### 1. Zero External Data Collection (100% On-Device Storage)
- **Local Storage:** All your family records, medicine schedules, appliance warranties, gold weights, and personal reminders are stored locally on your device via secure offline AsyncStorage.
- **No Cloud Server Tracking:** We do not upload your data to external servers. We do not track your activity, your device ID, or personal identifiers.
- **100% Ad-Free:** There are zero third-party advertisement SDKs or trackers in this application.

### 2. Device Permissions and Usage
The App only requests permissions strictly necessary to perform its core functions:
- **Location Permission:** Used locally in "School Bus / Live Trip" mode to calculate distance and estimate arrival times. Location coordinates are never sold or sent to any advertising servers.
- **Notifications:** Used strictly to deliver scheduled reminders (e.g., medicine dosage times, school bus departure, bill payments).
- **Camera & Photos:** Used only when you choose to scan/attach a document, invoice, or prescription to your local Family Vault.
- **Biometric (Fingerprint / Face ID):** Used strictly through the Android OS LocalAuthentication framework to protect your Family Vault on your phone.

### 3. Voluntary UPI Support / Contributions
- Voluntary donations or support contributions made through the App utilize standard Android UPI Deep Linking (`upi://pay`).
- The payment transaction occurs entirely within your chosen UPI application (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM).
- We do not capture, process, or store your bank account numbers, UPI PINs, or card details.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
MAST Life OS includes features for managing child profiles and school bus coordination. All information entered regarding children remains exclusively on the parent's device and is not collected or profiled.
