Privacy Policy — MAST Life OS

Effective Date: November 2026

MAST Life OS is built on a Zero-Server, Zero-Ad, Family-First philosophy. All your sensitive family data resides entirely on your device.

1. Data Collection & Storage

We do not store your data on external cloud servers. All personal documents, medicine schedules, appliance bills, and family notes are encrypted and kept locally on your smartphone.

2. App Permissions

  • Location: Required only for live school bus safety tracking.
  • Camera/Storage: Used exclusively to save document photos to your offline Vault.
  • Notifications: Used for punctual medicine and task alerts.
  • Biometrics: Used to unlock your personal family locker securely.

3. Payments & UPI Donations

All voluntary contributions are routed via standard Android UPI intent directly to your chosen payment app (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm). We never store your financial or banking credentials.
